# GREENLIGHT_REPORT.md template

Fill every `<…>`. Delete sections that would be empty, except the verdict and the matrix.
Link guideline IDs to Apple: `https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#<id>`. Drop the parentheses and lowercase the suffix: `5.1.1(v)` → `#5.1.1v`, `3.1.1(a)` → `#3.1.1a`.

## Scoring (use exactly this)

- Readiness score = 100 − 15 × BLOCKER − 7 × HIGH − 3 × MEDIUM − 1 × LOW (❌ FAIL items only, floor 0).
- Verdict: any BLOCKER → 🔴 **REJECTION LIKELY** · any HIGH → 🟡 **AT RISK** · otherwise 🟢 **GREENLIT**.
- 🔎 REVIEW and ❓ MANUAL items don't change the score but must be listed. A 🟢 with open MANUAL items says "Greenlit, pending N App Store Connect checks".
